Ingredients

tsp, tbsp and whole need build 15. Earlier builds render any unit they do not know as g, so a spoon would show as grams on a phone until then. Use g or ml for anything you want visible today.

Fill macros from looks the ingredient up in the app's own food catalogue and works out its macros from the quantity, so a recipe's numbers agree with what the same food logs as through search. It fills blanks only — anything you have typed is left alone. Needs a quantity in g or ml.

Macros are for the stated quantity of that ingredient in the whole recipe — not per 100 g, not per serving. Leave a macro blank if it is genuinely unknown; blank is not zero.
