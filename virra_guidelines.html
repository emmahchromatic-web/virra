A 60-second recap of who Virra is, who she's for, and what we're not. If everything else gets lost in a fire, this is what survives.
VIRRA · 04
§1.1 — Idea

Felt,
not
seen.

The brand idea.
The line that everything answers to.

Virra is the running brand for women who don't want to be looked at while they run — they want to feel something while they run.

Heat. Pulse. Pack. Mile. The somatic truth of the sport, not the optics of it.

We run hot.
We don't apologise.
We don't cool down
for anyone.

Every decision — colour, type, copy, casting, product — answers the same question: does this make her feel something? If not, it isn't Virra.

VIRRA. Felt, not seen.

VIRRA · 05
§1.2 — Pillars

Four pillars.
One heartbeat.

Brand pillars · §1.2
№.01

Pulse.

Embodied. Somatic. Alive. Heart rate as truth, not metric. Virra listens to the body before it counts the calories.

№.02

Heat.

Hormones. Effort. Flush. Fury. What diet culture taught her to fear, we treat as her superpower.

№.03

Pack.

A pack of one or a pack of a thousand. Women in motion together — never for the lens, always for the lift.

№.04

Mile.

Craft. Distance. Science. Earned every km, with training plans built around her physiology — not retro-fitted from his.

§2.3 — Spacing
2.3 · CLEAR SPACE

Give it
room to
breathe.

The clear space around the wordmark is 1× the cap height (X) on all four sides. Nothing — no copy, no edge, no other graphic — enters that zone.

X X
2.3 · MINIMUM SIZE

Don't go
smaller
than this.

Below these sizes the lowercase italic i loses its punctum and the wordmark falls apart. If you need it tinier, use the Pulse Mark or the V monogram instead.

Print 22mm Wide. Below this on cotton, vinyl, paper or signage, switch to the V monogram.
Digital 120px Wide. For UI, web, social headers. Below 120px, use Pulse Mark in place of the wordmark.
Favicon 16px Pulse Mark only. Never the full wordmark. The i disappears below 60px.
VIRRA · 10
§2.4 — DOs / DON'Ts

DOs and
don'ts.

Logo system rules.
If in doubt, ask.
DO Use approved lockups only. Lowercase italic Fraunces i is non-negotiable.
DO Pair with cream paper for editorial moments. Looks like a magazine.
DO On magenta, the i turns cream — never magenta-on-magenta.
DO Pulse Mark + wordmark may be combined. Mark sits left, wordmark right.
DON'T Don't skew, slant or rotate the wordmark. The italic i is the only italic moment.
DON'T Don't substitute the lowercase italic i with a regular i. The i is the brand, full stop.
DON'T Don't recreate the wordmark in any other typeface. Use the supplied SVG only.
DON'T Don't apply gradients or shadows. Virra colours are flat. Always.

Stencil.

Race banner · Tee back · Vinyl

Wordmark repeated, alternating caps and italic. Used at scale, not as wallpaper. For race kit, vinyl wraps, finish-line banners, the back of tees.

Contour.

Route maps · Editorial · Print

Topographic line work. Used as a soft pattern on educational content, route maps, and the back-of-the-magazine spreads. Always with a magenta dot for the runner.

Pulse.

App · Loading · Motion

Heart rate trace, alternating Pulse and Heat. Used in product UI, loading states, and as the brand's motion identity — animates left-to-right at the cadence of a long-run heart rate.

§06 — Photography

Photography.

Sweat is the light source. Real runners over models. The camera goes where the body is — never where the brand wants the body to be.
VIRRA · 22
§6.1 — Principles

Sweat is
the light
source.

Cast from clubs and parkruns, not agencies. Shoot in the conditions she actually runs in — pre-dawn fog, hard sun, the post-effort flush. Studio lighting is for product, never for the runner.

Light

Golden, hard, low. Pre-dawn blues. Post-rain wet sheen. Ring lights are forbidden.

Crop

Tight on hands on knees, sweat on collarbone, lungs working, mid-air feet. Symmetrical full-body retouch shots are not Virra.

Wardrobe

Lived-in kit. Mismatched club tees. The old shorts she keeps for long runs. No matching co-ords. No pastel.

VIRRA · 23
§6.2 — Casting

Cast for
truth,
not for type.

Real runners. Real bodies. Real cycle stages. We cast from the comments section before we cast from an agency book.

Bodies

All body types. Visible postpartum. Visible perimenopause. Visible scars. Visible joy.

Ages

18 to 65+. Every shoot includes at least one woman over 50.

Paces

4:00/km to 9:00/km. Pace is a number on a watch, not a cast requirement.

DO
  • — Real runners. Real club kit. Real club mud.
  • — Sweat, redness, breath, bodies in actual effort.
  • — Crop in: hands on knees, collarbone, mid-air feet.
  • — Hard or low natural light. Pre-dawn, golden hour, post-rain.
  • — Postpartum bodies. Perimenopausal bodies. Scars.
DON'T
  • — Before/after weight-loss frames, ever.
  • — Faceless "running into the sunset" clichés.
  • — Pastel matching co-ord sets. Manicured nails on the watch.
  • — Studio fluorescents. Ring lights. Retouched-smooth skin.
  • — Anything that asks her to perform femininity for the lens.
§07 — Voice

Voice.

A coach who is also her best friend. Warm. Direct. Never cute. Names the thing. Roars in caps when it matters.

Templates · product, push, plan.

Working voice library.
Copy and adapt.
Product · Plan opener

Day before
a long run.

"Tomorrow's long run. You don't need to feel ready. You need to lace up. We've trained for this."

Don't: "You've got this, queen!" / "Smash it tomorrow babe!" — anything cheerleader-coded.
Push notification · Pre-dawn

06:14
nudge.

"It's still dark. The bed is warm. Lace anyway. The first km is the hardest part of the day."

Don't: "Rise & grind!" / "No excuses!" — bro-fitness language is forbidden.
Product · Recovery day

Rest is
training.

"Today is a no. Not because you're weak — because you're listening. Walk. Sleep. Eat. We'll run tomorrow."

Don't: "You missed today!" / "Streak broken." — never punish rest, never gamify recovery.
Product · Cycle-aware coaching

Cycle d.21
callout.

"You're cycle-strong this week. Use it. Push the threshold session. Trust the body that's doing the maths."

Don't: "Hormones can be tough!" — never patronise her physiology, treat it as data.
Push · Post long run

You
did it.

"That was a hard one. Eat. Rest. Brag a little. The pack saw it too."

Don't: "You crushed it!" / "Beast mode!" — performative rather than felt.

Before
day one.

"No plan yet. That's a fine place to start. Pick a distance, pick a date, we'll do the maths."

Don't: "Get started today!" / "Begin your journey!" — bland onboarding-speak.

Templates · social, email, errors.

Voice in the wild.

Social · Manifesto frame

Pillar
post.

"Your cycle is not a bug. It's a performance variable. We treat it like one."

Don't: hashtag stack, "#fitspo #girlboss" — Virra doesn't beg for an algorithm.
Social · Confessions / education

I thought

Don't: bait-and-switch hook copy. The format is honest, not click-y.
Email · Welcome

First
email.

"You're in. Here's what we're not: a diet plan, a step counter, a girlboss pep-talk. Here's what we are: training, body literacy, a pack."

Don't: "Welcome to our journey!" / "Let's crush your goals!" — opt-in cliché.
Email · Race week

Five
days out.

"Race week. Don't pick up new tricks. Don't read more articles. Don't second-guess your shoes. Just turn up."

Don't: "You've trained, now believe!" — vague platitudes are the enemy of good copy.
Error · Connection lost

Offline.

"Connection lost. Your run isn't. We'll catch the data when you're back."

Don't: "Oops! Something went wrong!" — Virra speaks like an adult, not a chatbot.
Error · Plan paused

Plan
paused.

"Plan paused. Whenever you're ready — illness, life, just because — we'll pick it back up where you left it. No streak to break."

Don't: "Don't lose your progress!" — never use loss-aversion to coerce a return.
