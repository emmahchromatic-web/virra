§01 — Context

Why this site.

Why virra.app exists, what it has to do, and what it explicitly is not.

Background

VIRRA is a female-first running platform in development. The full app — cycle-aware training, female-first nutrition, run logging, female health education — is in its foundations phase and not ready to ship. The website goes first, in parallel, as the work the app can't yet do: build trust, capture audience, generate enquiries.

What the website has to do

  • Grow the newsletter. Run Hot — the renamed VIRRA publication (replacing "Confessions of a Corporate Runner") — is the primary owned audience while the app is being built. Subscribers join The Pack.
  • Build credibility. Show the science-informed, female-first thinking behind VIRRA before users are asked to pay for it.
  • Generate 1:1 coaching enquiries. Coaching is live now and is the income bridge while the app is built.
  • Earn organic traffic. Useful tools (pace calculator) and evergreen content do the SEO heavy lifting.
  • Carry the brand. The site is the first place outside Instagram that VIRRA's voice and design system live in public.
  • Be ready to evolve. Components, routes and CMS choices should hold when "Get the App" replaces "Subscribe" as the primary CTA in 6–12 months.
§02 — Brand

Felt, not seen.

The brand idea everything answers to. If a design choice doesn't make her feel something, it isn't VIRRA.

The idea

VIRRA is the running brand for women who don't want to be looked at while they run — they want to feel something while they run. Heat. Pulse. Pack. Mile. The somatic truth of the sport, not the optics of it.

Four pillars

  • Pulse. Embodied. Heart rate as truth, not metric.
  • Heat. Hormones, effort, flush, fury. Treated as a superpower, never apologised for.
  • Pack. Women in motion together. Never for the lens, always for the lift.
  • Mile. Craft, distance, science. Earned every km — built around her physiology, not retrofitted from his.

Voice

  • Don't use floating labels. Labels always sit above the input.

§06 — Editorial & design principles

Non-negotiables.

If a design or copy choice fails one of these, it isn't VIRRA. Cut it.
№.01
Felt, not seen
Design for how it makes her feel. Big type, flat colour, no gradients, no stock-photo running shoes.
№.02
Female-first, never diet-culture
No calorie shaming, no weight-loss framing, no "bikini body" anywhere — including alt text and meta copy.
№.03
Body as data
Treat physiology — cycle, HR, hormones — as performance variables. Never as feelings to manage.
№.04
Speak like an adult
No "crush it", "beast mode", "let's get ready to crush your goals". No hashtag-stack social copy. Honest hooks only.
№.05
Built for the founder to update
If Emma can't change copy, swap an image, or publish an article without the developer, the build isn't done.
№.06
Anticipate the app
Components, design tokens and routes should still hold when "Get the App" becomes the primary CTA.
