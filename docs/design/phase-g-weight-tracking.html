Virra doesn't track weight, it narrates it. Every reading is interpreted through three lenses: cycle (luteal water retention, follicular baseline), training (glycogen restoration after long runs, inflammation post-race), and fuelling (TDEE vs intake projection variance). The chart's annotations turn dots into memory — "this peak was post-Brighton, normal" — so the user reads their own pattern as the story it actually is. Foresight defuses the diet-culture reflex.
