# Virra Content Publishing Guide

A step-by-step guide for writing and publishing blog posts on virra.app.

---

## How it works

Posts are written in the CMS at **virra.app/admin**. When you publish, the CMS saves your post to GitHub and the site rebuilds automatically — the post goes live within about 60 seconds.

---

## Getting started

### First-time login
1. Go to **https://virra.app/admin**
2. Click **Login with GitHub**
3. Authorise the Virra CMS app when prompted
4. You'll land on the CMS dashboard

You'll need a GitHub account that has been added as a collaborator on the `emmahchromatic-web/virra` repository. Ask a developer to add you if you can't log in.

---

## Writing a new post

### 1. Create the post
- Click **New Blog Posts** in the sidebar
- A blank post form will open

### 2. Fill in the fields

**Title**
The headline. Keep it under 70 characters for SEO. Example: *Five Ways to Run Faster Without Training More*

**URL Slug**
The web address for the post. Use lowercase letters and hyphens only — no spaces or special characters.
- Good: `five-ways-to-run-faster`
- Bad: `Five Ways to Run Faster!`

The slug will be automatically combined with the date to form the final URL, e.g. `/blog/2026-05-10-five-ways-to-run-faster`.

**Excerpt**
A short summary (10–200 characters) used on the blog index page and in search results. Write it like a search result snippet — what would make someone click?

This is **not** shown inside the article itself.

**Hero Paragraph**
A single compelling paragraph (20–400 characters) shown at the top of the article in italic display font, like a magazine standfirst. Think of it as the TLDR for the reader — something that draws them into the full piece.

Example: *We tested six popular recovery methods over eight weeks. The results surprised us — and they'll change how you think about rest days.*

**Cover Image**
The main image for the post. Shown on both the blog index card and at the top of the article.
- Use a 16:9 image (landscape)
- Minimum size: 1200 × 675px
- Click the image field to upload directly from your computer

**Category**
Choose one:
- Fitness
- Nutrition
- Performance
- Wellbeing
- App Updates

**Tags** *(optional)*
Comma-separated keywords. Example: `running, recovery, training`. These appear at the bottom of the post.

**Author**
Select **Virra Team** (additional authors can be added by a developer).

**Published Date**
Defaults to today. You can set a future date to schedule a post — it will go live on the next site build after that date.

**Body**
The full article content. The editor toolbar has all the standard formatting options:
- Headings (use H2 and H3 — H1 is reserved for the post title)
- Bold, italic
- Bullet and numbered lists
- Blockquotes (great for pull quotes)
- Links
- Images (uploaded inline)

---

### 3. Save and publish

- **Save** (top right) — saves a draft. The post is not yet live.
- **Publish now** (dropdown next to Save) — publishes immediately. The post goes live within ~60 seconds.

---

## Editing an existing post

1. Click **Blog Posts** in the sidebar
2. Find the post and click it to open
3. Make your changes
4. Click **Publish now** to push the update live

---

## Adding images to the body

In the body editor, click the **image icon** in the toolbar. You can:
- Upload a new image from your computer
- Enter a URL to an image hosted elsewhere

Images should be high resolution and relevant to the content. The site will display them at full column width.

---

## Tips

- **The post is not live until you click Publish** — saving only stores a draft
- **After publishing, wait ~60 seconds** then refresh the blog to see your post
- **Don't change the URL slug after publishing** — it will break any existing links to that post
- **Hero paragraph ≠ excerpt** — the excerpt is for search/index cards, the hero paragraph is the first thing a reader sees inside the article. Write them differently
- **Proofread in the preview** — use the Preview button (eye icon, top right) to see roughly how the post will look before publishing
